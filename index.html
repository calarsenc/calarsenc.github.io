I enjoy talking to and helping anyone interested in Substrate Independence for Structural AI Safety.

I am Co-Founder and President of Netholabs. We build recording systems, cross-species datasets, and foundation models of living organisms. Our systems record continuous neural and behavioural data from living mammals, including humans. We use that data to model how biological systems perceive, learn, adapt, and act over time.

Modern AI is trained on the outputs of intelligence: language, code, images, video. There is almost no data on the dynamics that produce intelligence in the first place. We are making those dynamics a training substrate. Today we apply this to the capability and safety of frontier AI and robotics. As the measurements get richer, the same path leads to functional models of whole organisms.
